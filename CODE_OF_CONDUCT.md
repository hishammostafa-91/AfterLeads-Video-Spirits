# Code of Conduct

AfterLeads-Video-Spirits is built by people from many countries, languages, and backgrounds. We want every participant — users, translators, testers, and developers — to feel welcome and respected.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive. Assume good intent.
- Respect different languages and levels of experience; explain rather than dismiss.
- Give feedback on work, not on people.
- Accept decisions gracefully once they are made, and raise new information respectfully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, stalking, or unwanted contact.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic posting.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at afterleadseg@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this code. Decisions take into account the severity and any repetition of the behavior.
